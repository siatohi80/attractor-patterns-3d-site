Put the pattern on

Click a face on the model to choose where the pattern goes, and Shift-click to add faces that touch it. "Wrap" lays the pattern along the surface like a decal, so spacing stays even on curved and steep parts. Top, Front, Side and View project it straight along one direction instead, like a slide projector; parts steeper than about 70° to that direction stay plain.

How it works

Open a model or pick a sample. The pattern is laid out flat on the model's footprint, seen from the projection direction, and projected onto the faces that look that way. Place attractors by clicking on the model: each opening grows or shrinks with its distance to them, just like the 2D playground.

Cut out removes the openings: an open surface gets holes, a closed solid is cut right through. Extrude raises the openings by the depth, and Recess sinks them, with side walls, so a closed solid stays closed. Apply several times to layer patterns, and use Undo to step back.

Sizes are in millimetres. Exports are triangle meshes.
